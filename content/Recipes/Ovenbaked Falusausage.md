### Ingredients
**The Sausage Dish**
- 1 Whole Falusausage
- 1x Onion
- 1-2x Can of Crushed Tomatoes
- Paprika
- (Optional) Apple Slices 
- (Optional) Boston Cucumber 
- (Optional) Roasted Tomato Slices
- Mustard
- Shredded Cheese
- Spices (Black Pepper and any other you like)

**The Side**
- Rice OR
- Potatoes
- Milk
- A little Butter

### Preparation
**Preparing the Sausage Dish**
- Grind down the Onion in a mixer.
- Mix the Onion and Crushed Tomatoes in a oven pan.
- Put the Sausage in the pan .
- Cut slices almost through the sausage, top to bottom.
- Fill the gap in the slices with Tomatoes, Paprika, Apple Slices or Cucumber.
>[!tip] Randomize what you put in each slice for some extra fun around the dinner table!
- Shred Cheese and sprinkle over the sausage and tomato paprika sauce
- Sprinkle over some spices to finish off the dish
- Put the dish in the oven until the cheese has melted and gone golden.

>[!question] Be experimental, you can swap out the Paprika for other things!

**While the Sausage is in the Oven**
- Boil the Potatoes in a pot
- Pour out the water when they have boiled enough and began softening
- Mash them in the pot
- Add milk and some butter
- Stir the mix on low heat for a few minutes

### Serving
Server with Rice or Mashed Potatoes!
