A typical and beloved Thailandish dish that's become loved all over the world. A great dish for people that crave Asian cuisine but without the heat!

### Ingredients
**Main Ingredients
- 3x Chicken Fillet
- Noodles
- 2x Onion

**The Sauce**
- Fish Sauce
- Oyster Sauce
- Chinese Soy
- 3x Garlic Slices
- Spring Onion
- Water
- A squeeze of Lime
- Chili (as much as you want)
- Pea Shoots
- 2x Eggs

### Preparation
**The Chicken**
- Slice the Chicken Fillets
- Fry the Chicken and Onion in a Wok pan with some Oil. Fry until the Chicken has gotten color

**The Sauce** 
- Mix these ingredients together in a pot:
   - Water
   - The Lime juice
   - Soy
   - Oyster Sauce
   - Fish sauce
   - Finely sliced Garlic
   - Finely chopped Chili
- Mix in the the noodles, spring onion and pea shoots
- Fry the sauce for a few minutes
- Break the eggs into a bowl and whip gently
- Pour the eggs into the sauce
- Fry the sauce until the eggs have settled

### Serving
Pour the sauce over the now woked Chickens and Onions. Add some crushed nuts on top if you're not allergic.
