### Ingredients
- 1x Butternut Squash
- 1 Garlic Onion
- 1x Onion
- 1x Celery
- ½ Lime
- Olive Oil
- Salt
- Smoked Paprika Spice

### Preparation
- Cut the Butternut Squash in half
- Drizzle over some Olive Oil and Salt
- Roast the Squash in the oven
- Scoop out the Squash into a pot
- Add some water
- Add some Oil, the Garlic, Celery and Onion
- Cook everything until it's well mixed
- Add the Smoked Paprika spice and any other Seasoning you like
- Salt as needed
- Add a squeeze of Lime
- Let simmer on low heat for a few minutes

### Serving
Server with Buttered Bread or any other sides you like with a soup.
