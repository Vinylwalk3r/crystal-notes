A taste of the Swedish kitchen. Meat and cabbage all boiled to a fine stew and served with potatoes and lingonberry jam.

### Ingredients
- 1x Cabbage Head
- 1x Minced Meat (about as much weight as the cabbage head)
- As many Potatoes as you like
- Spices (Mexican Spices and Meat & BBQ spices)
- Vegetable Broth
- Meat Bouillon Cube

### Preparation
- Prepare the potatoes and begin boiling them

- Cut the cabbage head into small pieces
- Boil the cabbage inside a pot with water
- Fry the minced meat in a pan with some butter
- Sprinkle some spices onto the meat
- After the meat is finished, add it into the cabbage
- Add the Vegetable Broth and 2-3x Meat Bouillon Cubes (add Bouillon cubes as needed)
- Add water if needed

### Serving
Serve with the potatoes and lingonberry jam
